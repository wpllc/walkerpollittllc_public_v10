Walker Pollitt LLC applies systems engineering discipline to facility assets, MEP telemetry monitoring, workflow automation, and high-consequence decision controls.

Grounded in systems engineering methods and technical documentation standards, our operations focus on providing practical systems-decompositions, evidence validation procedures, and spreadsheet/reporting automation scripts that help complex operations remain safe and reliable.
